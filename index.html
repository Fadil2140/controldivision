ABOUT COMPANY

One partner for integrated building control.

PT Wira Kusuma Sejahtera has been established since 1997. Since 2012, we have developed an integrated approach to HVAC solutions, with a particular focus on chiller control systems and building solutions.

Starting in 2019, the Control Division expanded its focus to HVAC control & automation systems, energy management, monitoring, metering, and various building facilities.

Our Vision

  1. To be Trusted, Effective and Efficient.
  2. To become an integrated and sophisticated control systems provider, focused on Control, Monitoring and Optimization.

Our Mission

  1. Build control systems that are integrated with various other systems.
  2. Develop sophisticated and modern control systems technology.
  3. Improve control systems to manage data and information.

OUR SOLUTIONS

Designed around your building.

From the air side to the water side, we integrate control, monitoring, and optimization into a single, easy-to-manage system.

HV

HVAC Control

Control and monitoring for AHU, VRF, operating theaters, temperature, humidity, pressure, and ventilation systems.

CPC

Chiller Plant

Sequencing, temperature-based control, trending, data logging, and alarm history for water-cooled and air-cooled equipment.

EN

Energy Monitoring

Real-time monitoring of electricity, water, and gas consumption, cloud connectivity, and energy usage analysis.

PLC

PLC & Automation

PLCs, modular I/O, HMIs, gateways, and communication via Modbus, Ethernet, MQTT, and other industrial protocols.

IoT

IoT & SCADA

Web-based monitoring, remote access, cloud engine, and data integration for centralized control.

⚙

Engineering & Support

Engineering, procurement, installation, programming, commissioning, training, and technical support.
